Seen on every Dr. Martens heel loop, “With Bouncing Soles” has gone from referencing its trademark air-cushioned sole to encompassing the countless communities who wear their Docs to express their individuality.

The defining slogan now headlines a new global platform exploring how it has transcended meaning in culture. To celebrate its launch, the brand joined photographer and visual artist Sophia Wilson for a special New York City event. The Bouncing Soles experience spotlighted her unique creative voice and how she stays inspired through style, girlhood, and an overarching sense of togetherness.

In a look back at the event, Hypebeast dives into her story in dedicated chapters. Resonating with many other creatives in the city, we find out what makes soles like Wilson’s truly bounce today.

SOPHIA'S NOTES

Sophia's notes on the night

Conceptualized by Manon Macasaet and Raquel Michel, a New York City skyline set the scene for the event, illustrating the place that many creatives call home. The backdrop was paired with a Polaroid wall, where guests could put their mark on the space.

Continuing the customization theme, a charm-making bar allowed guests to craft bespoke Crazy Horse leather key rings with unique charms. From sweet treats and smiley faces to trinkets of iconic landmarks, each piece drew on Wilson’s playful lens of the city.

Lounge areas brought a joyful energy to the space, styled with tactile furry sofas and bold striped pillows so guests could kick back, enjoy a fresh matcha from Matchaful, take in DJ Memphy’s set, and get inspired while making their charms.

My work is all about girlhood. Every facet embodies my experience growing up as a girl one way or another.

The vibe was perfect, colorful, delicious, energetic, and fabulous.

A great artist can convey their aesthetic and point of view – but doesn’t feel the need to stick to just one medium. Don’t forget to put yourself out there as much as possible because you never know who’s watching.

Girlhood defines your entire life growing up as a woman; even when you’re old, you’re still growing up, and I think that’s really beautiful.

I love putting little charms on my bags, shoes, and keys, so I thought the charm bar would be perfect for my Dr. Martens With Bouncing Soles experience.
